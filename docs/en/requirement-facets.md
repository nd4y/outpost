## Identical input in independent domains

`TestIndependentOwnerDomainsIdenticalInput` directly covers the storage-domain data-key facet of REQ-07, the no-cross-domain-deduplication facet of REQ-14 and the domain-access facet of REQ-38. Two independently created owners back up identical bytes under the same path and explicit file identity. Their encrypted dependency sets must be disjoint. Each pre-exported kit must restore with its own read-only owner material after both original stores and source files are removed; the kits contain only `kit.json` and ciphertext objects.

Crossed owners must fail to open the marked stores and to restore the bare kits. A separate variant keeps the correct domain and verification key, passes signed-envelope admission and then substitutes the other domain's data key. Both refusal paths must leave no output or plaintext stage. Native CI selects the named case on amd64 and arm64. The existing single-owner, different-file confidentiality test remains separate.

This bounded test establishes the named cryptographic and independent-kit assertions. It does not establish tenant roles, quota isolation, network placement or real destination admission. Those facets retain their own checks and acceptance evidence.

The [HDD receipt](../domain-isolation-evidence.json) records four passing groups and three subtests on frozen source: the new case passed in 1.92 seconds, alongside existing confidentiality, alias classification and source hardlink-denial controls. The 0.5 CPU / 512 MiB container peaked at 199,630,848 bytes without memory-limit, OOM or swap events. Its earlier run remains failed: the related source test rejected the launcher's temporary root before capture. Compiling that test separately and executing it without `GOTMPDIR` preserved the volume guard and passed on the same test source. Native execution and whole-product acceptance retain separate evidence.

## All authorized ingress unavailable

`TestSourceAllIngressUnavailablePreservesHeadAndBoundedCache` separates total ingress refusal from successful standby selection. After a complete generation, both configured authenticated receivers refuse readiness. Repeated attempts and agent reopen must retain the exact signed checkpoint, operation and generation, admit no payload and preserve the regular-file cache inventory. The old version restores locally during the outage. When the primary returns, exactly the next generation completes; both versions then restore after source removal. The small fixture declares a 2 MiB logical cache bound. These are distinct cache/admission facets of REQ-04/05 and bounded measurement facets of REQ-44/53. They do not establish full-size physical allocation, all capacity planning, three independent failure domains or whole AT-29 acceptance.

## Recovery export after mount namespace restart

`TestSourceRecoveryExportAcrossMountNamespaceIDChange` checks the owner-export facets of REQ-08 and the source-root admission facets of REQ-48 with selected tools. A saved namespace-local mount ID may differ after restart while the filesystem, FSID, root inode and read-only state remain the same. Export must pin a fresh observation for the current operation, retain the signed completed checkpoint and refuse changed stable identity or replacement during export. Owner/runtime/source separation and private output permissions remain mandatory. The test requires an independently selected built distribution; ordinary tests without it skip this case, while native candidate jobs explicitly provide it.

## Telemetry transport outage and return

`TestTelemetryTCPOutageReopenAndSameTargetRecovery` directly checks the durable telemetry queue of REQ-42 and the sensitive-data exclusion facet of REQ-43. A closed TCP listener must leave the accepted request pending; reopening the outbox must preserve its exact signed journal, cursor and retry deadline. When the same authenticated endpoint returns, it must receive the original request. Arbitrary warning or rejection diagnostics must remain absent from persisted content and operator status. The original byte/item bounds still apply.

The [HDD receipt](../telemetry-outage-evidence.json) records 30 passing top-level observations across ordinary and race execution, including the outage case and separate SIGKILL/slow-sink controls. These remain component assertions. They do not establish elapsed retry timing, SIGKILL durability, real Collector interoperability, host-wide resource accounting or whole AT-26 acceptance. Those properties retain separate checks and evidence.
